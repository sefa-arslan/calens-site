Support

Questions, bugs or data requests? Write to us and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

calens@oda.so

If the email link does not open, copy the address into your mail app.

Please do not include meal photos, Health data, birthdates, account tokens or purchase receipts in a support request.

Purchases and Billing

Apple handles billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds for App Store purchases. To manage or cancel, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone.

If a purchase doesn’t show up in Calens, we can help you restore it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel?

Apple manages all billing. On iPhone, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Calens, then follow the steps to cancel. Deleting the app or deleting your local data does not cancel anything you bought through Apple.

A purchase doesn’t show up in Calens.
  1. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used for the purchase.
  2. In Calens, go to Profile > Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
  3. Check your connection, then close and reopen Calens.
  4. If the purchase is waiting for approval (for example, Ask to Buy), it appears after it is approved.

If it still doesn’t appear, email us with your device model, iOS version and the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all refunds for App Store purchases. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Calens support can’t issue or approve refunds.

The AI photo analysis failed.

Try these steps:

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Make sure the food is clearly visible, centered and well lit.
  • For packaged food, use Barcode or Food label instead.
  • Check that AI meal analysis is on in Profile > Settings > Privacy.

If Calens asks you to wait, try again in a few minutes.

Is the AI estimate accurate? Is this medical advice?

AI results are automated estimates and can be incomplete. Review and edit the values before you save a meal. Calens is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. For health or diet decisions, talk to a qualified professional.

How do I connect Apple Health, or why is no Health data showing?

Apple Health is optional and read-only: Calens reads active energy, workouts, steps, exercise minutes and weight, and never writes to Apple Health. This data stays on your device.

  • Go to Profile > Settings > Health and tap Open Apple Health Integration.
  • If you declined access earlier, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then, under Privacy, tap Apps > Calens and turn on the data types.
  • Data appears when iOS makes it available, which can take a few minutes.
How do I export or delete my data?

Your profile, meals, photos and progress stay on your device. Calens keeps no server copy of them.

  • Export: Profile > Settings > Local data > Export My Data.
  • Delete: Profile > Settings > Local data > Delete local data. This removes your local profile, meals, photos and progress from this device.

Deleting local data does not cancel anything you bought through Apple. Meal photos sent for AI analysis are not stored; they are discarded after the analysis.

How do I change the app language?

Go to Profile > Settings > App Language. Calens is available in Arabic, Chinese (Traditional), Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish and Turkish.

Which units does Calens use?

You can enter your height and weight in metric or imperial units during onboarding. Calens shows energy in kilocalories (kcal) and weight in Progress in kilograms (kg).

Does Calens show ads?

No. This version of Calens does not show ads.
