Support

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Need help with local data export or removal, an App Store purchase, privacy, or accessibility?

Email calens.support@gmail.com. If the email link does not open, copy the address into your mail app. Support requests are sent through your chosen mail app rather than an in-app support-message form.

For subscription billing, renewal, cancellation, or refunds, manage your subscription through your Apple account. Calens support can help with app access and restore questions, but Apple handles App Store billing decisions.
