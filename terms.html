Terms of Use

Last updated: October 31, 2026

These terms apply to your use of Calens. By using the app, you agree to use it responsibly and only where permitted by law.

Age Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to use Calens. Calens is not intended for children or minors.

Nutrition and Wellness Information

Calens provides nutrition estimates and tracking tools for general wellness awareness. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional health advice.

Your Information and Content

You are responsible for the information you add to Calens and for keeping your device and, where applicable, account access secure. Meal photos, profile data, and logs should only be added if you have the right to use them.

Food Data and Third-Party Sources

Calens may display barcode, label, nutrition, and ingredient reference information from app-owned datasets and Open Food Facts. Open Food Facts database material is made available under the Open Database License, with attribution and share-alike obligations for reused database material. Product images and other contents may have their own license terms. You should review and edit food data before relying on it in your log. Calens does not guarantee that third-party product data is complete, current, official, or suitable for medical decisions.

Free Use and AI Photo Analysis Allowances

Manual logging, barcode lookup, nutrition label scanning, the food library, progress review, and Apple Health features are free. AI photo analysis runs on a paid cloud service, so its use is limited:

  • Free: one successful AI photo analysis in total. This is a one-time allowance, not a daily one.
  • Calens Plus: up to 30 successful AI photo analyses per day while the subscription is active.
  • AI credits: once your Free or Calens Plus allowance is used up, each additional successful AI photo analysis uses one AI credit.

A failed analysis does not count toward your allowance or use a credit. AI results are estimates; review and edit them before saving.

Calens Plus Subscriptions and Auto-Renewal

Calens Plus is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store and available as a monthly or yearly plan. The price, currency, and billing period are shown on the purchase screen and in the App Store before you confirm.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase or, if you start a free trial, when the trial ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period at the then-current price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple Account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Free trial: the yearly plan may include a 1-week free trial for eligible new subscribers (one introductory offer per Apple Account). If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the trial converts automatically to a paid yearly subscription and you are charged the yearly price. The monthly plan has no free trial.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions). Canceling stops future renewals; you keep Calens Plus until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Apple handles billing, renewals, and refunds. To request a refund, use Apple's process at reportaproblem.apple.com. Calens cannot issue refunds directly.
  • Restore Purchases in the app re-checks your active subscription with the App Store. No sign-in is required.

Removing local Calens data does not cancel an active App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account settings if you no longer want it. Any price, duration, trial, renewal, or cancellation terms shown by Apple at the time of purchase control the transaction.

AI Credit Packs

AI credit packs (10, 30, or 100 credits) are consumable App Store purchases. The price and currency are shown in the App Store before you buy. One credit covers one successful AI photo analysis, and credits are used only after your Free or Calens Plus allowance is used up.

  • Credits do not expire. A failed analysis does not consume a credit.
  • Credits are non-refundable except where Apple grants a refund under its own policies; request any refund through Apple.
  • Credits are not transferable or redeemable for cash.
  • Your credit balance is kept on the Calens server. Credits are linked to a random ID in your iCloud Keychain, so they follow you to devices signed in to the same Apple Account when iCloud Keychain is on; otherwise they stay with that device. Because credits are consumable, Restore Purchases does not recreate them, and they are lost if you erase the device while iCloud Keychain is off.

Apple Terms

Your use of Calens from the App Store is also subject to Apple Media Services terms and applicable App Store rules. Apple is not responsible for Calens maintenance or support.

Account Deletion

Settings lets you remove app-owned local data; no Calens cloud account or sign-in exists. Deleting local data does not cancel a subscription and is not an App Store refund. Manage subscriptions or request any eligible refund through Apple.

To prevent stale credentials or duplicate Store transaction delivery from recreating deleted data or value, Calens retains a deletion block for no more than 24 hours and a versioned, purpose-scoped HMAC deletion marker for less than 72 hours. Limited Store owner-state metadata is removed within the same period. A field-minimal, owner-unlinkable replay fence may then remain for the life of the service; it cannot recreate a profile or credit balance and contains none of the account, App Account Token, product, or transaction-link fields listed in the Privacy Policy.
