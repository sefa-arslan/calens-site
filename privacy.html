Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Calens works without a Calens account. Your profile details, goals, meal entries, progress logs, and saved photos stay in the app's storage on your device. This policy explains those on-device features and the few online services Calens uses: AI photo analysis and its allowance, barcode lookup, and app analytics.

Information You Provide

You may enter profile and planning details, meal items, portions, nutrition values, notes, and progress information. You can also save photos for supported local features. These records are stored on your device and are not uploaded to a Calens account. Support opens your chosen mail app; Calens does not receive message contents unless you choose to send an email.

Health and Fitness Data

If you enable Apple Health in Settings and grant permission, Calens reads active energy, workouts (type, duration and calories), steps, exercise minutes and body mass to show your daily activity and calories burned on device and for optional weight import. The permission request is read-only; Calens does not write Health data. You can change Health access in iOS Settings or Apple Health. Health values never leave your device and are not sent to Calens servers, analytics, or any third party.

Food Lookup and Photos

Barcode lookup sends the barcode needed for a product search and standard request metadata to Open Food Facts. Food-label text recognition runs on device; you review and confirm a draft before it is saved. Barcode and label information may be incomplete or outdated, so review and edit it. Open Food Facts data is subject to its published licenses, including attribution and share-alike terms for reused database material. When you choose Analyze, Calens sends the selected meal photo, plus any optional notes, in one request through Firebase Functions to Google Vertex AI for analysis; it is not stored on a Calens server. The photo is discarded after analysis, including when analysis fails; it is never written to storage. Calens does not keep meal history, AI responses, optional notes, or saved meal images in the cloud.

Online AI Services

For AI meal analysis, the selected photo and any optional notes are sent inside a single encrypted request to Firebase Functions, which passes them to Google Vertex AI for the estimate. No account or user identifier is created; Firebase App Check confirms the request comes from the genuine Calens app. The photo is not uploaded until you tap Analyze, is never written to storage, and is discarded after the response.

AI Photo Analysis Allowance

AI photo analysis costs money to run, so its use is limited: the Free plan includes one successful AI photo analysis in total, Calens Plus includes up to 30 successful AI photo analyses per day, and AI credits cover analyses beyond that. A failed analysis does not count. To apply these limits without an account, Calens keeps only the following, and none of it identifies you:

  • Free analysis: Calens uses Apple's DeviceCheck service. With each AI photo analysis request, the app sends a temporary token generated by Apple; the Calens server passes it to Apple to read or set one flag that Apple keeps for your device ("free analysis used"). Calens does not store the token and never learns a device identifier. Apple keeps the flag until Calens resets it, including after you delete and reinstall the app.
  • Calens Plus: the app sends the Apple-signed record of your current subscription. The server checks Apple's signature and stores only a keyed one-way hash of the subscription's original transaction ID, today's analysis count, and the date. These counters are deleted after about 40 days.
  • AI credits: the app creates a random ID, keeps it in your iCloud Keychain, and attaches it to credit purchases through the App Store. The server verifies each Apple-signed credit purchase once and stores a keyed one-way hash of the random ID with your remaining credit balance, plus a keyed one-way hash of each credit purchase's transaction ID (kept up to two years) so a purchase is never added twice.

Calens does not store raw transaction IDs, your Apple Account, name, email, IP address, or advertising identifier for these limits, and does not use them for analytics, advertising, or tracking. If Apple's App Store Server API is enabled, the server may also ask Apple whether a subscription is still active (for example after a refund).

Purchases

Apple handles App Store billing and transaction processing. Calens uses StoreKit for subscription status and AI credit packs. The app sends Apple-signed purchase records to the Calens server only to apply the AI photo analysis allowance and credits described above. Apple may retain purchase records under its own terms and legal obligations.

Optional AI credit packs are consumable App Store purchases. Credits do not expire, are used only after your Free or Calens Plus allowance, are not transferable or redeemable for cash, and are not recreated by Restore Purchases. Your credits are linked to the random ID in your iCloud Keychain, so they follow you to devices signed in to the same Apple Account when iCloud Keychain is on; otherwise they stay with that device. No sign-in is required.

Analytics and Diagnostics

Calens uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand how people use the app, for example whether onboarding is completed, which plan screen was shown or closed, whether the first AI photo analysis was completed, and whether a trial, subscription, or credit pack was purchased. Events carry only event names and simple categories or counts (such as which screen showed a plan offer). Firebase Analytics uses an app instance ID created by Firebase and Google may derive an approximate region from the IP address.

Calens does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the vendor identifier (IDFV) for analytics, turns off ad personalization and ad-network signals, does not link analytics to your identity, and does not use analytics for advertising or tracking. Calens never sends meal details, Health values, photos, notes, profile data, payment details, or transaction IDs to analytics. Crashlytics collection is turned off. Analytics data is retained according to Google's Firebase settings for the Calens project.

Advertising

This release of Calens does not show ads. No ad requests are made, no ad privacy message is shown, and no age-range question is asked. If a future release turns advertising on, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that release.

App Privacy Summary

Data used by Calens is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking: meal photos and optional notes (AI analysis, not retained), purchase records and the anonymous IDs described above (AI photo analysis allowance), and app interactions with an app instance ID and approximate region (analytics).

Subscriptions

In-app purchases are managed through Apple and the App Store. Any price, renewal, cancellation, or refund terms shown by Apple at purchase control the transaction. Deleting local Calens data does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage it in your Apple Account settings.

Retention

Local content remains in the app container until you remove it with Delete local data or remove the app. Calens keeps no server copy of your profile, history, photos, or AI results.

The AI photo analysis allowance keeps only the keyed counters described above: Plus daily counts for about 40 days, credit balances while credits remain, and credit purchase hashes for up to two years. Apple keeps the DeviceCheck flag for the device. Delete local data does not remove purchased credits, so a deletion is never a silent loss of paid value.

Your Choices and Requests

Use Delete local data in Settings to remove app-owned records and photos from your device. Deleting local data does not cancel an App Store subscription or create an App Store refund. Because the allowance counters are keyed one-way hashes that Calens cannot link to you, Calens cannot look them up by name or email; contact support if you have a question about them.
