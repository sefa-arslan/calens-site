What Calens Does, In Plain Words

What it is
An iPhone app that turns a photo of your meal into calories and macros, then lets you check every ingredient before it goes into your day.
Ways to log
Snap a meal photo for an AI breakdown, scan a barcode for packaged food, or read a printed nutrition label right on your iPhone.
What it tracks
Calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, saturated fat and sodium for every meal, measured against your daily calorie and macro target.
Progress
Weight against your goal, a day streak and before-and-after photos side by side. Connect Apple Health to add steps, workouts and calories burned.
You stay in control
See the ingredients the AI found, change an amount or add a hidden one like oil or sauce, and the nutrition values recalculate on the spot. Nothing is saved until you approve it.
Look back
A day-by-day food diary, saved meals you can add again in a tap, and a PDF report of any period to share.
Languages
The app is available in 12 languages: English, Arabic, Chinese (Traditional), Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish and Turkish.
Platform
iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

FAQ

Your Questions, Straight Answers

Questions, bugs or data requests? Write to us and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

calens@oda.so
Which devices does Calens support?

Calens is made for iPhone and runs on iOS 17 or later.

Which languages does Calens support?

The app is available in English, Arabic, Chinese (Traditional), Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish and Turkish. You can switch languages inside the app.

Where are my meals and photos stored?

On your iPhone. Your profile, meals, photos and progress are saved in the app, not in a Calens account. You can export a copy or delete everything in Settings.

What happens to my meal photo?

It leaves your iPhone only when you tap Analyze. The photo is sent for that one AI analysis and discarded afterward; it is not stored on a server. Barcode and label scans don’t need a meal photo.

How accurate is the AI?

It does the heavy lifting, and you have the final say. Results are estimates, not lab measurements. Portions and hidden oil or sauce are the hardest to see, so Calens shows every ingredient it found and lets you change an amount or add what it missed before you save.

Does Calens work with Apple Health?

Yes, if you turn it on. With your permission, Calens reads active energy, workouts, steps, exercise minutes and weight to show your daily activity and calories burned. It only reads, never writes, and your Health data stays on your iPhone.

Does Calens give medical advice?

No. Calens helps you log food and follow your own goals. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

How do I get help?

Email calens@oda.so and include your iPhone model and iOS version.
